## Notes for Trainee QA
When filling this template:
1. Be specific and factual - avoid opinions
2. Include exact steps to reproduce
3. Attach screenshots showing the exact point of failure
4. Check if bug still exists before reporting (not a one-time issue)
5. Verify it's not a known issue before reporting
6. Include environment details - many bugs are environment-specific
